Privacy Policy

Word Streak Duels · Instant Game by Apex Arcade Studio
Last updated: 10 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Streak Duels (“the Game”), operated by Apex Arcade Studio (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play on Facebook Instant Games / Messenger.

1. Who we are

Word Streak Duels is a casual word puzzle Instant Game published by Apex Arcade Studio, an independent developer studio. Studio site: Apex Arcade Studio.

Contact for privacy questions: use the contact email registered on the Facebook app dashboard for this game, or the email on the Apex Arcade Studio Meta Business portfolio.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you play, the following may be processed:

  • Facebook Instant Games platform data provided by Meta (for example player ID, name, locale, and context) through the Instant Games SDK. We use this only as needed to run the Game (play, share, scores, ads).
  • Gameplay data stored on your device via browser local storage (for example daily best score, streak, streak freezes, mute preference). This stays on your device unless you clear site data.
  • Share content you choose to send (for example score and seed text) when you use Share score.
  • Advertising data if you watch a rewarded ad (streak freeze). Ads are provided by Meta Audience Network and subject to Meta’s policies.

3. How we use information

  • To provide and improve the Game experience
  • To save personal bests and streak progress on your device
  • To enable share / challenge features you choose
  • To show optional rewarded ads you choose to watch
  • To understand stability and basic usage via Facebook Instant Games analytics

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not require a separate account or classic Facebook Login
  • We do not collect payment card details in the Game

5. Third parties

The Game runs on Meta / Facebook Instant Games. Meta may process data under their own terms and privacy policy when you use Facebook or Messenger. Rewarded ads use Meta Audience Network.

Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

6. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience consistent with Facebook’s platform rules. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

7. Data retention

Device-stored scores and settings remain until you clear them or uninstall / clear browser data. Platform data is retained according to Meta’s Instant Games policies.

8. Your choices

  • You can stop playing at any time
  • You can decline rewarded ads (you simply won’t earn a freeze from that offer)
  • You can clear local game data in your browser / device settings
  • Facebook account and ad preferences can be managed in your Facebook settings

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued play after updates means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about Word Streak Duels, contact the developer through the email listed on the Facebook app settings for this Instant Game, or via Apex Arcade Studio’s Meta Business contact details. Studio home: https://aipagesnow.github.io/Facebook-Games/
