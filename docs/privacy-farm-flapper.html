Privacy Policy — Farm Flapper

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Farm Flapper is a casual arcade game from Apex Arcade Studio. It may run as a Facebook Instant Game and/or an Android app.

What we store

  • A display name you type (optional) and your personal best, on your device.
  • If you use the Instant Game, Facebook may provide a first name via the Instant Games SDK. We do not use classic Facebook Login.
  • If an online leaderboard is enabled, your name and score may be stored on our hosting (for example Vercel) so the board can be shown to other players.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not require an account for a local game.
  • v1 does not include ads. If ads are added later, this page will be updated and the store ads declaration will match.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
