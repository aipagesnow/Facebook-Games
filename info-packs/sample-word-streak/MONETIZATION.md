# Monetization

## Model

**Hybrid: rewarded ads primary + light cosmetic / convenience IAP.**

## Placement rules

1. Never show ads during active typing / timer.
2. Rewarded only after explicit player choice (hint, freeze, extra ladder).
3. If interstitial is used: only between sessions, max frequency cap, skippable when policy allows.

## Suggested economy

| Action | Cost |
|--------|------|
| Hint (next letter) | Rewarded ad or soft currency |
| Streak freeze | Rewarded ad (1/day free after ad) |
| Extra daily ladder | Rewarded ad or IAP bundle |
| Tile skin pack | IAP |

## Fun-kill checks

- Can a player complete core loop forever without paying? **Yes.**
- Does social challenge require IAP? **No.**
- Are ads skippable choices rather than walls? **Yes.**
