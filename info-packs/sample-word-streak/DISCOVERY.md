# Discovery assets guidance

## Category

Word / Puzzle (confirm exact Instant Games category list in developer console at upload time).

## App icon (1024×1024)

- Large letter tiles (e.g. W · S · D)
- High contrast, flat lighting, no tiny text
- Readable when scaled to ~48–64px

## Cover (1600×300)

- Left: playful letter chain animation still
- Center: title wordmark
- Right: two simplified avatars + vs badge
- Avoid clutter; sell “friends + words + speed”

## Share / result image concepts

- “I chained 12 words from CRANE — beat me”
- Streak badge + seed word of the day

## Video (optional but strong)

- First 2 seconds: finger typing first word
- Second 2 seconds: score pop + challenge button
- Keep under ~15s for completion

## Store text angles

- Hook: “60-second word duels with friends”
- Feature bullets: daily ladder, streaks, one-tap challenges, Zero Permissions ready
