# Audience

## Primary

- Facebook / Messenger users who already play casual Instant Games.
- Age band roughly 18–44 (platform skew may be broader).
- Motivation: quick wins, friend bragging rights, daily habit.

## Session context

- Opened from Gaming Tab, Messenger thread challenge, or share link.
- Often on mid-range mobile; must feel instant on 4G.

## Messaging that works

- “Beat my word chain”
- “Daily ladder is live”
- “Don’t break your streak”

## Messaging to avoid

- Hardcore crossword / Scrabble mastery framing
- Long narrative setup
- Pay-to-progress that blocks social play
