# Audience — Android

Casual Android players 18–44 who already like word / puzzle Play titles and want a 2–4 minute session.

- **Context:** commute, couch, waiting room. Portrait one-hand.
- **Not for:** children (avoid Designed for Families), hardcore crossword fans, multiplayer- obligatory social.
- **Messaging:** “60-second daily word ladder. Beat your best.”
