# Filter decision — Word Streak Duels (Android)

## Stage 1 — Hard gates

| Gate | Verdict |
|------|---------|
| Short-session, ad-friendly, Play-policy-clean | **Pass** — 30–90s rounds, rewarded ads after a scored round |
| Small team can ship an AAB | **Pass** — wrap existing HTML5; do not rewrite native |
| Play listing + Data safety is realistic | **Pass** — local scores + optional AdMob advertising ID |

## Stage 2 — Opportunity

Play has room for a **fast daily word ladder** that is not a Words With Friends clone. Differentiation is onboarding speed and a clean ad moment, not a new dictionary mechanic.

## Stage 3 — Execution

Live-ops burden is medium-low (daily seeds, theme weeks). Signing, Play forms, and AdMob are the real v1 work — not a new game design.

**Decision:** `ready` as a **game**.
